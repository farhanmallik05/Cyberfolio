# SEED-003: 3D Elemental Fidelity & High-Res Geometry

## Context
During Phase 9 (Multi-theme hardening), we identified that the 3D structures (AICore, BackgroundSystem) required higher geometric resolution and deeper thematic "elemental" physical properties.

## Requirements
- **Rounding**: Increase Sphere segments to `128` and Icosahedron detail to `5` for "fully round" appearance.
- **Arctic (Ice)**: Implement "frozen" physics—low distortion, high clearcoat, near-zero roughness.
- **Inferno (Fire)**: Implement "plasma" physics—high-speed aggressive distortion, matte heat-shimmer roughness.

## Trigger
- **Surface at**: Phase 11 (Cinematic Home Build).
- **Reason**: This is the major UI milestone where visual flair is the primary focus.
