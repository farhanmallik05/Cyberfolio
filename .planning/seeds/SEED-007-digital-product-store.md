# SEED-007: Digital Product Store (Expanded)

## What
Beyond templates — a full digital product store selling: video tutorials/mini-courses, n8n workflow JSON exports, Figma component libraries, AI prompt packs, code snippets/boilerplates.

## Why
Turns passive portfolio traffic into recurring revenue. Positions you as an expert and educator, not just a practitioner. Each product is also a marketing artifact that attracts your target audience.

## Details
- Product categories:
  - n8n Workflow Packs (automation ready-to-import)
  - Figma UI Kits (the Neural Architect design system)
  - AI Prompt Packs (curated for developers and builders)
  - Code Boilerplates (Next.js starters, Firebase setups)
  - Mini-Courses (recorded, asynchronous)
- Payment: Gumroad embed or custom Stripe integration
- Product pages: description, preview, what's included, testimonials
- Free samples: lead magnet to grow email list
- Marketplace page (already in nav) becomes the storefront

## Trigger Condition
Surface when beginning **Milestone 3: Monetization** and after at least 2 products are ready to sell. Don't build the store before you have inventory.

## Implementation Notes
- Gumroad: easiest to start (iframe or redirect links)
- Custom Stripe: more control, better UX, requires backend
- Firebase already set up — can store product metadata and track downloads
- Email list: integrate ConvertKit or Resend for lead magnet delivery
