# 3. Strict Chat Scope Guard

We decided to enforce a strict defensive guard on the AI chatbot's persona. 

The chatbot must decline answering any queries that are not directly relevant to Farhan Mallik's professional background, skills, projects, services, or tools. 

This prevents cost and quota exhaustion from general-purpose usage (e.g. users requesting coding assistance or general knowledge queries) and keeps the chatbot aligned purely with lead generation and conversion.
